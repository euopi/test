Atlas Drill

Pick a region, then find every country in it. You get three tries per country; the map colours each one by how many tries it took.

Game mode

Borders: Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 0 (de facto boundaries; Somaliland and Northern Cyprus are drawn as part of Somalia and Cyprus). Each map is a Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection centred on its region, so shapes and relative sizes stay true. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Small countries also get a round marker you can click.
